Nothing bolted on.

Questions.

When does the iPhone 18 Pro ship?

Released 18 September 2026 in more than 65 countries and regions, following pre‑orders that opened on 12 September.

What actually changed from iPhone 17 Pro?

The A20 Pro chip on 2 nm, the first physical variable‑aperture camera in an iPhone, a smaller Dynamic Island, a larger Pro Max battery, and 5G over satellite on the C2 modem. The aluminium unibody design carries over.

Is there still a black Pro?

Yes — Black returns for the 18 Pro, alongside Burgundy, Glacier and Silver.

Which modem is in the Pro Max?

Apple's C2 everywhere except the United States, where the Pro Max uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon X80.
